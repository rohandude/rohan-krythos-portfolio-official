Habit of a Warrior

Build Habits. Break Weaknesses.

Build powerful habits, track runs, and level up your warrior every day!

Habit of a Warrior transforms your daily habits into an exciting RPG-inspired journey. Complete habits, track your running, earn XP, level up your warrior, and build consistency one day at a time.

Whether your goal is fitness, productivity, studying, or self-improvement, every completed habit makes your warrior stronger.

Features

  • ⚔️ Gamified habit tracking
  • 🏃 GPS run tracking
  • ⭐ Earn XP and level up
  • 🔥 Daily streak system
  • 🎯 Create and manage custom habits
  • ⏰ Powerful habit reminders and alarms
  • 🛡️ Warrior profile with progression
  • ☁️ Cloud backup for premium users
  • 🏆 Leaderboards and achievements
  • 🌙 Clean, modern dark theme

Discipline is your greatest weapon. Every habit completed is another step toward becoming the strongest version of yourself.
Start your warrior journey today!
